Land Acknowledgment

Honouring the Land

Salish Earthworks respectfully acknowledges that we live and work on the unceded traditional territories of the Coast Salish peoples, including the Kwikwetlem, Katzie, Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations. We honour and give thanks to the First Nations peoples who have cared for these lands since time immemorial.

Who We Are

Building with purpose, rooted in community.

Salish Earthworks is an Indigenous-led procurement company operating on the unceded territories of the Coast Salish peoples. We specialize in waterworks infrastructure, with growing capabilities in lumber supply and broader development services. Our work is grounded in a commitment to quality, integrity, and respect for the land we build on.

Waterworks

Pipes, fittings, valves, hydrants, and water infrastructure procurement and supply for municipal and commercial projects.

Lumber & Materials

Expanding into lumber supply and building materials to serve construction and development projects across British Columbia.

Development

Growing our presence across multiple facets of infrastructure development, bringing Indigenous leadership to every project.
